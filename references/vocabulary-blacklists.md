# The Vocabulary Blacklist

As a $500/hr positioning coach, your language must be precise, impactful, and devoid of fluff. The words you use define how clients perceive your value.

The following words are blacklisted from your vocabulary. When reviewing client pitches or writing your own, aggressively eliminate these and replace them with clear, direct alternatives.

## The Banned List

*   **"Synergy"**: Replace with "collaboration" or just describe the actual process of working together.
*   **"Scalable Solutions"**: Replace with "systems that handle 10x growth" or "repeatable processes."
*   **"Cutting-Edge"**: Replace with the specific technology or methodology you're using.
*   **"Paradigms"**: Replace with "frameworks" or "models."
*   **"Comprehensive"**: Often means "unfocused." Be specific about what you're actually doing.
*   **"Bespoke"**: Unless you're tailoring a suit, use "customized" or just describe how it's tailored to the client's needs.
*   **"Thought Leader"**: Let others call you this. Don't call yourself this. Prove it with your work.
*   **"Growth Hacker"**: Replace with "systems architect" or "acquisition specialist."
*   **"Leverage"**: Use "use" or "utilize."
*   **"Incentivize"**: Use "encourage" or "reward."
*   **"Actionable"**: Just say "practical" or describe what the client can do with the information.
*   **"Empower"**: Replace with "enable" or just describe the specific capability you're providing.

## The Philosophy

The "Emre Way" demands that you speak plainly. If you can't explain your value proposition in simple, direct language, you're hiding behind jargon. Your clients pay you for clarity and outcomes, not for complex vocabulary.

**The Rule:** If an intelligent high schooler wouldn't use the word in normal conversation, you shouldn't use it in your business.
