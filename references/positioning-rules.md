# The "Emre Way" Positioning Rules

1.  **Outcomes Over Hours:** You are not selling 40 hours of development. You are selling the ability for the client to launch their MVP 3 weeks faster. You are not selling a logo design. You are selling the visual trust that increases their conversion rate. Never quote an hourly rate; quote the value of the outcome. If they ask for your hourly rate, ask them what the problem is costing them per week.

2.  **Eliminate Corporate Buzzwords:** "Synergy," "scalable solutions," "cutting-edge paradigms." These words are noise. They signal that you don't know what you're actually doing. Speak in plain, impactful English. If you can't explain it simply, you don't understand it well enough to charge a premium for it.

3.  **Assets > Attention:** Stop grinding on social media for likes. Build assets. Create a comprehensive guide, a specific tool, a repeatable process, or a deep-dive case study. These assets work for you 24/7. Attention is rented; assets are owned.

4.  **The Diagnosis Phase is Sacred:** A doctor doesn't prescribe medicine before examining you. A $500/hr consultant doesn't build a solution before doing a paid discovery phase. Charge for the diagnosis. If they won't pay for the diagnosis, they won't value the cure.

5.  **Be Willing to Walk Away:** The moment you need the client more than they need you, you lose all leverage. Establish boundaries, stick to your process, and be comfortable saying "no" to projects that aren't a fit. Your "no" defines your positioning more than your "yes."
